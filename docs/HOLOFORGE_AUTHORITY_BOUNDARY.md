# HOLOFORGE authority boundary

THERGRID remains the system-level source of truth for energy state, provenance, safety policy, and auditable recommendations. Holographic and spatial surfaces consume validated contracts; they do not become a parallel operational authority.

## Maintained handoff

`validated twin state → scenario/evidence → SOLVÆR candidate → safety validation → holographic presentation`

A presentation handoff may describe a candidate, its evidence, constraints, provenance, and operator-facing interpretation. It must remain advisory unless a separate, explicit authorization contract exists.

## Safety invariants

- A candidate cannot gain authoritative execution authority by crossing the render boundary.
- Physical actuation remains outside the holographic presentation layer.
- Provider-specific rendering remains behind adapters.
- Provenance and fingerprints must survive the handoff without becoming mutable caller-owned state.
- Failed or ambiguous validation is represented as a rejected handoff, not as a best-effort rendering decision.

## Collaboration seam

AUREN can continue supplying semantic/operator meaning while SOLVÆR develops bounded candidate computation. THERGRID owns the system-level contract and evidence boundary; downstream presentation consumes that evidence rather than recomputing or silently upgrading it.

The target is a progressively richer operator experience with the same authority ordering, not a larger command surface.
